
This is my Project Purpose Small Store Inventory
The purpose of the Small Small Inventory System is to help small business owners organize and manage their products efficiently.
It aims to make tracking product quantities, monitoring available stocks, and managing inventory records easier and more convenient.

Planned Features
Product Management – Add, edit, and delete product information, including product name, category, and price.
Stock Monitoring – Track available product quantities and monitor stock levels.
Low Stock Alerts – Identify products that are running low and need restocking.
Inventory Search – Search and find products easily by name or category.
Inventory Reports – View product lists, total quantities, and inventory value.
